My name is Kehan Wu, and I am currently a second-year master's student in Electronic Science and Technology at the National Graduate College for Engineers, Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), where I rank 1st out of 16 students with a GPA of 3.65/4.00. I am writing to express my strong interest in your research and my sincere enthusiasm for pursuing a PhD under your supervision.

My research interests lie in integrated sensing and communication (ISAC), wireless sensing, IoT, multimodal perception, and deep learning. Over the past three years, I have been working in the LASSO Lab under the supervision of Assoc. Prof. Rui Wang. During this time, I have been deeply involved in several research projects related to wireless sensing, human activity perception, and sensing system development, which further strengthened my motivation to pursue academic research.

My current main project focuses on cross-domain wireless human motion sensing for mmWave human activity recognition. In this work, we aim to reduce the dependence on large-scale labeled real-world radar data by developing a simulation-to-reality and cross-orientation sensing framework. Specifically, I developed a data augmentation pipeline that takes a small number of single-view video sequences as input and generates radar sensing data from multiple viewing angles. Based on the augmented data and real-world radar measurements, we further achieved action classification and orientation recognition on measured multi-action and multi-angle mmWave radar data. Through this project, I gained hands-on experience in physics-guided sensing simulation, cross-modal data generation, radar signal processing, and real-world sensing system deployment. I also contributed to building the end-to-end ISAC sensing and communication platform, including the ZED 2i simulation-to-real measurement system and the real-world data collection pipeline.

Previously, I led a project on human respiration detection under interference in a mmWave ISAC system, which resulted in a first-author publication at WCNC 2024. In addition, I contributed to a separate project accepted by AAAI 2026, where we studied ultrasound image segmentation using a neural architecture search-optimized Vision Transformer framework. These experiences have allowed me to develop a broad background in wireless sensing, deep learning-based perception, and multimodal signal analysis.

I have attached my CV for your reference. I would be very grateful if you could let me know whether you are currently considering recruiting PhD students, and whether my background may be a good fit for your group.

Thank you very much for your time and consideration. I sincerely look forward to hearing from you.

Sincerely,  
Kehan Wu
